# Food Waste Analysis Project

## Project Overview

Food waste is one of the biggest operational challenges in the restaurant industry. Poor inventory management, inaccurate demand forecasting, overproduction, and expired products can all lead to unnecessary food waste. As a result, restaurants face higher operating costs and reduced profitability.

Restaurant management has identified food waste as a key business issue but lacks a structured process for collecting, transforming, and analyzing operational data. Consequently, it is difficult to determine which products generate the most waste, identify the main causes of food waste, and understand which business processes require improvement.

## Project Goal

This project simulates a real-world Data Engineering scenario where raw operational data is transformed into a clean and reliable dataset for analysis.

The data pipeline makes it possible to: 
- Analyze food waste patterns. 
- Identify the main sources of food waste.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
- Improve inventory management. 
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Provide actionable recommendations to reduce food waste.
